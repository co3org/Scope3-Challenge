## Offset vs. Inset

| | Offset | Inset |
|-|--------|-------|
| Location | Outside value chain | Inside value chain |
| Scope 3 attribution | No | Yes (specific category) |
| Additionality | Distant, hard to observe | Closer, tied to commercial relationship |
| SBTi treatment | Restricted to residual | More likely to count |
| Current market price | $2–40/tCO₂e (volatile) | Not yet liquid |

## The Decision Checklist

Use this to pressure-test any Scope 3 claim — yours or someone else's.

**Measurement**
- [ ] Are the material categories identified, or is everything spend-based?
- [ ] Is primary supplier data used where the footprint is largest?
- [ ] Are emission-factor sources and geographies disclosed?

**Credibility**
- [ ] Is the baseline real and audited, or is "avoided emissions" being claimed?
- [ ] Is additionality demonstrated (financial, regulatory, barrier tests)?
- [ ] Is there independent third-party verification (ISO 14064-3)?

**Action**
- [ ] Is there a financing mechanism, or just an engagement target?
- [ ] Does the reduction flow back to a specific Scope 3 category?
- [ ] Does it survive CSRD assurance (audit trail, no double counting)?

**Strategy**
- [ ] Is the GHG Protocol revision outcome being tracked?
- [ ] Are hard-to-abate suppliers funded, or just exited?

## Key Takeaways

- If you remember one thing: **Scope 3 is 70–90% of the footprint, and measuring it is not reducing it.**
- If you remember two: **offsets are out, insets are in — but inset accounting is not yet settled.**
